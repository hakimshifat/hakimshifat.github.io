## Thoughts

Andy Weir at his best. A thrilling sci-fi adventure that celebrates science, problem-solving, and unlikely friendships. It's funny, tense, and deeply moving.

## What Struck Me

- The **scientific accuracy** (or at least, the feeling of it) in every solution.
- The **dynamic between the main characters** (Amaze!).
- The **optimistic tone** even in the face of planetary extinction.

## Favorite Moments/Quotes

> "You're a scientist, Ryland. Figure it out."

## When I Read It

Finished in 2024. One of the best books I've read in years.
