## Thoughts

A hilarious and heartwarming sitcom about a group of detectives in Brooklyn. It manages to balance absurdity with real emotional depth and social commentary perfectly.

## What Struck Me

- The **ensemble cast** has incredible chemistry.
- How it handles **serious topics** with both humor and respect.
- **Captain Holt's** character development is legendary.
