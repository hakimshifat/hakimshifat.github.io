## Thoughts

Watched the movie based on *Kichukkhon* by Humayun Ahmed. It captures the essence of the characters and their complex relationships beautifully.

## What Struck Me

- The **vibrant atmosphere** of the train journey.
- The **psychological depth** typical of Humayun Ahmed's works.
- How the **intertwined lives** are portrayed in such a short span.

## Favorite Moments/Quotes

> "Some journeys are more about the people we meet than the destination itself."

## When I Watched It

Watched recently in this march of 2026. A touching story that stays with you.
Gonna read the book soon when i forget the movie.
