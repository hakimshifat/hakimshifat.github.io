## Thoughts

An unparalleled journey of adventure and world-building. It's not just about the destination; it's about the bonds formed along the way and the pursuit of dreams against all odds.

## What Struck Me

- The **incredible world-building** that spans over 1000 episodes.
- How **deeply emotional** even the minor character backstories are.
- The **central themes of freedom** and inherited will.

## Favorite Moments/Quotes

> "People's dreams... never end!"

## When I Watched It

Caught up with the latest episodes before 2025. It's been an epic ride.
