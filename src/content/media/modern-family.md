## Thoughts

A refreshing take on the family dynamic. The mockumentary style adds a layer of honesty and humor that makes the characters feel like real people you'd want to know.

## What Struck Me

- The **intertwined families** and how they support each other.
- **Phil Dunphy's** "Phil's-osophy".
- The **clever writing** that ties all subplots together in each episode.

## Favorite Moments/Quotes

> "When life gives you lemonade, make lemons. Life will be all like 'What?!'"
