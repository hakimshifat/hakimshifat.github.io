## Thoughts

A sweeping epic that transcends the anime genre. It starts as a simple story of humanity vs. monsters and evolves into a complex exploration of war, cycles of hatred, and the cost of freedom.

## What Struck Me

- The **moral ambiguity** of every character.
- The **unfolding mystery** that stays engaging for four seasons.
- The **stunning animation** and haunting soundtrack.

## Favorite Moments/Quotes

> "If you don't fight, you can't win."

## When I Watched It

Finished in December 2024. A masterpiece that left me speechless.
