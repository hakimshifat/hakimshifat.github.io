## Thoughts

A masterpiece of mockumentary storytelling. It captures the mundane reality of office life and turns it into something both incredibly awkward and deeply moving.

## What Struck Me

- **Michael Scott's** desperate need for validation.
- The **Jim and Pam** slow-burn romance.
- The **cringe humor** that is somehow endearing.

## Favorite Moments/Quotes

> "That's what she said!"

## When I Watched It

Finished in 2024. A show that gets better with every rewatch.
