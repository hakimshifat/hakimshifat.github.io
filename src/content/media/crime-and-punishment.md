## Thoughts

Raskolnikov's descent into guilt and redemption—a psychological exploration of morality, suffering, and the human capacity for change. Dostoevsky's masterpiece is as relevant today as it was in the 19th century.

## What Struck Me

- The **intensity of the internal monologue** of Raskolnikov.
- The **moral dilemma** at the heart of the story.
- How the **atmosphere of St. Petersburg** reflects the protagonist's mental state.

## Favorite Moments/Quotes

> "Power is given only to those who dare to stoop and take it."

Finished in December 2024. A truly profound reading experience.
