# Campus Ambassador @ GeeksforGeeks

## Overview

Serving as the primary point of contact between GeeksforGeeks and Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP). My role involves bridging the gap between the student community and industry-standard resources provided by GeeksforGeeks.

## Responsibilities

* Act as the official representative and primary point of contact for GeeksforGeeks on campus.
* Promote GeeksforGeeks courses, events, and coding resources to the student body.
* Organize and coordinate technical events, seminars, and coding competitions in collaboration with GeeksforGeeks.
* Foster a strong technical community and encourage peer learning among students.

## Key Goals

* Increase awareness and engagement with competitive programming and technical skill development platforms.
* Build a robust network of tech enthusiasts within the university.
