# Co-Founder and Security Researcher @ [Cyb3r Invasi0n Army (CIA)](https://ctftime.org)

## Overview

Founded in August 2025 by BUP students driven by curiosity, precision, and passion for cybersecurity, Cyber Invasion Army (also known as Team Uchiha) was born to challenge limits and master the art of problem-solving under pressure. We live by the Uchiha spirit — discipline, determination, and a relentless will to learn. 

## Key Achievements

* **Global Ranking:** Ranked 49th globally overall with 349.718 pts in 2026.
* **National Ranking:** Ranked 3rd in Bangladesh in 2026.
* **Top Placements:** Secured top 50 positions in highly competitive international CTFs including UMDCTF (21st), CTF@AC26 (22nd), TexSAW (22nd), and PsychCTF (37th).

## The Vision

We learn by breaking. We defend by understanding. Every challenge sharpens our skills, every exploit teaches us more. We aim to build not just a team, but a legacy of excellence in cybersecurity.

Cyber Invasion Army — forged in BUP, powered by passion, and focused on the next challenge. August 2025 was just the beginning.
